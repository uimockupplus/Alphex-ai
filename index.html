06 / Trust, security + responsible AI

Trust starts with how the system is built.

We design for visibility and control from the start. Specific security controls and data handling are agreed for each project and deployment.

Designed for oversight

  • Clear system boundaries
  • Grounded, approved knowledge
  • Defined escalation paths
  • Human oversight where needed
  • Observable system behavior
  • Ongoing evaluation

Security considerations

  • Data handling scoped to the project
  • Access designed around the use case
  • Responsible deployment planning
  • Validation before consequential actions
  • Privacy requirements discussed early
  • Controls documented with the client

We do not claim certifications or universal security guarantees here. Controls, hosting, retention, and access requirements should be confirmed for the specific implementation.

07 / Industry solutions

AI shaped around the work of your industry.

Different industries have different knowledge, customer needs, and operating rules. Alphex starts with those realities, then scopes assistants and workflows to fit. These are example applications—not claims of existing customers or pre-built certified solutions.

Knowledge + service workflows

Financial services

Help teams find current product information, policy guidance, and approved service procedures without searching across disconnected documents.

Example applications
Internal policy and product knowledge assistants; routine customer FAQ responses; service-request intake and routing. Advice, eligibility decisions, and other consequential actions stay with authorized people.
Administrative support

Healthcare

Make approved administrative information easier for staff and patients to navigate, with clear boundaries around clinical matters.

Example applications
Appointment and service FAQs; staff access to internal procedures; administrative request routing. Clinical guidance and patient-care decisions require qualified human oversight.
Product + customer experience

SaaS + technology

Connect product documentation and support knowledge so customers and employees can get useful, consistent answers.

Example applications
Documentation-grounded support assistants; onboarding and feature guidance; ticket summarization and triage; internal engineering or sales knowledge search.
Expertise + repeatable delivery

Professional services

Help teams reuse internal expertise and handle repeatable research and delivery steps while keeping professional judgment in the loop.

Example applications
Search across approved methods and project materials; research and document drafting support; proposal knowledge retrieval; workflow checklists and handoffs.
Commerce + customer operations

E-commerce

Support shoppers and service teams with product knowledge and routine order-related information across the customer journey.

Example applications
Product and policy FAQs; order, delivery, and return information; service-request capture; routing exceptions to a person rather than guessing or promising an outcome.
Cross-team operations

Growing businesses

Give lean teams a practical way to make shared knowledge available and reduce manual steps as the business grows.

Example applications
A shared internal knowledge assistant; lead and customer inquiry handling; repeatable onboarding and operations workflows; lightweight integrations with existing tools.

Can it work with our existing tools?

Usually. We start with the systems your team already uses and scope the integrations around the workflows that matter.

How long does a first version take?

A focused FAQ assistant can be ready in a few weeks. Timeline depends on your content and the number of channels.

Small and mid-sized businesses with repetitive customer questions and a real need for dependable automation.
