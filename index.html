FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

What does Alphex AI do?

Alphex AI provides AI data and annotation services, AI evaluation, custom AI application development and intelligent workflow automation.

Do you work with AI startups?

Yes. We aim to support AI startups and development teams with data preparation, evaluation and AI-related project requirements.

Can you evaluate an existing AI chatbot?

Yes. We can assess AI responses using defined evaluation criteria and structured quality review.

Can you build custom AI applications?

Yes. We develop applications such as knowledge assistants, document intelligence tools and other AI-powered solutions based on project requirements.

Do you provide annotation services?

Yes. Our service capabilities include image, video and multimodal annotation, alongside evaluation and quality assurance.

How is project pricing determined?

Pricing depends on project scope, workload, technical complexity, integrations and delivery requirements.

Can you integrate with our existing systems?

Integration possibilities are assessed based on your existing applications, APIs, data sources and project requirements.

Do you provide ongoing support?

Support and maintenance can be included in the engagement depending on the agreed scope.

Have an AI challenge worth solving?

Tell us what you are building, what needs improvement or which business process you want to transform. Let’s explore the right solution together.
