08 / FAQ

Good questions.
Clear answers.

Have a question about a use case or integration? Tell us what you’re trying to solve.

Talk to Alphex ↗
Will an AI assistant make things up?

No system can guarantee perfect answers. We can ground responses in approved sources, test edge cases, define uncertainty behavior, and route sensitive situations to a person.

Can it work with our existing tools?

Usually, depending on the tools and their available integrations. We review your current workflow and scope the connections around the task.

How long does a first version take?

A focused first version may take a few weeks. Timing depends on content readiness, integrations, testing requirements, and the number of channels.

How do you approach data and security?

Data handling, access, hosting, retention, and deployment controls are discussed and scoped for each project. We don't assume one configuration fits every business.

Can you improve an AI system we already have?

Yes. We can review the current experience, identify quality gaps, and help with evaluation, knowledge grounding, workflow design, or targeted improvements.
