ALPHEX AI / FAQ
What kinds of AI systems does Alphex build?

We build AI assistants, knowledge search systems, workflow automations, custom integrations, and evaluation tools tailored to practical business needs.

How do you keep answers grounded?

We connect the system to relevant, approved business information and design ways to surface uncertainty and hand work to a person when appropriate.

Can an AI assistant take actions in our tools?

Where a use case requires it, systems can connect with approved tools. Actions and confirmation requirements are scoped around the workflow.

How long does a first version take?

Timeline depends on the workflow, source material, integrations, and review needs. We agree on a practical scope before work begins.

How do we get started?

Tell us what your customers or team need help with. We’ll discuss the workflow, requirements, and a sensible first step.
