09 / FAQ

Good questions.
Clear answers.

Have a question about a use case or integration? Tell us what you’re trying to solve.

Will an AI assistant make things up?

No system can guarantee perfect answers. We ground responses in approved sources, test edge cases, define uncertainty behavior and route sensitive situations to a person.

Can it work with our existing tools?

Usually, depending on the tools and their integrations. We review your workflow and scope the connections around the task.

How long does a first version take?

A focused first version may take a few weeks, depending on content readiness, integrations, testing and channels.

Do you also build websites?

Yes. Alphex Studio designs and builds websites, landing pages, online stores and web apps, and can add an AI assistant when you’re ready.

Can you improve an AI system we already have?

Yes. We review the current experience, find quality gaps and help with evaluation, grounding and workflow design.
