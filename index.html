05 / FAQ

Good questions deserve clear answers.

Will it make things up?

We keep answers grounded in approved sources, test risky replies, and hand off when confidence is low. We make the limits visible.

Can it work with our existing tools?

Usually. We start with the systems your team already uses and scope the integrations around the workflows that matter.

How long does a first version take?

A focused FAQ assistant can be ready in a few weeks. Timeline depends on your content and the number of channels.

Who is Alphex for?

Small and mid-sized businesses with repetitive customer questions and a real need for dependable automation.
