04 / FAQ

Good questions deserve clear answers.

What is Alphex AI?

Alphex AI is the parent company behind products focused on AI evaluation, AI quality, and the broader AI workforce.

What is EvalLoop AI?

EvalLoop AI is an AI evaluation and quality platform designed to help assess AI responses, multimodal outputs, safety, RAG systems, conversations, annotations, and other AI behaviors.

What is EvalLoop Jobs?

EvalLoop Jobs is an independent opportunity discovery platform. It curates opportunities across AI evaluation, data annotation, AI training, GenAI, prompt engineering, agentic AI, and related areas. It is not an employer.

What is coming next?

We are continuing to build EvalLoop AI, including benchmark testing and additional evaluation capabilities for different types of AI systems.
