03 / AI systems

How an AI system comes together.

A production AI solution is usually more than a model. It combines models with data, instructions, business logic, interfaces, integrations, and operational safeguards.

MODEL

The AI model generates, interprets, classifies, summarizes, reasons over, or transforms information based on the selected use case.

CONTEXT

Relevant information, instructions, conversation history, or business context supplied to the system so it can produce more useful results.

KNOWLEDGE

Business information that can be retrieved and provided to an AI system when answers need to be grounded in specific sources.

TOOLS

External capabilities such as APIs, databases, search systems, business applications, or other services that an AI workflow may use.

WORKFLOW

The sequence of actions, decisions, validations, and human interactions required to complete a business task.

INTERFACE

The place where users interact with the system, such as a website, chat interface, internal tool, application, or business workflow.

05 / Implementation

Designed for your existing environment.

Alphex AI solutions can be designed around an organization's existing technology rather than requiring every business to replace its current systems.

Depending on the project, an implementation may connect with existing websites, applications, APIs, databases, knowledge repositories, communication tools, or internal workflows.

The specific architecture depends on the business requirements, technical environment, data sensitivity, expected usage, and operational constraints of each project.

06 / Quality

AI systems need a quality bar.

AI outputs can vary. A useful AI implementation therefore requires more than simply connecting an application to a model.

Depending on the project, we may evaluate areas such as:

  • Factual accuracy
  • Instruction following
  • Relevance and completeness
  • Grounding in approved information
  • Safety and appropriate behavior
  • Consistency across common use cases
  • User experience and workflow fit
  • Failure and edge-case handling

The exact evaluation approach depends on the system and the consequences of incorrect output.

07 / Security

Security and responsible implementation.

Security considerations should be incorporated into the design of an AI system rather than treated as an afterthought.

Depending on the project, considerations may include authentication, authorization, data access, secrets management, logging, input validation, model access, third-party dependencies, and protection of sensitive information.

Specific security controls and responsibilities should be agreed according to the requirements and risk profile of the project.
