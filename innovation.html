07 / FAQ

Good questions.
Clear answers.

Not sure whether your idea or situation fits? Just ask us.

Talk to Alphex ↗
Is this a job, internship or investment?

No. The Innovation & Collaboration Program is not a job opening, paid internship or investment program. It’s a completely free opportunity to explore ideas, collaborate and build practical technology solutions together, with no fee or investment required.

Do I need a company or an existing product?

No. You don’t need an established startup, company or product. Individuals, students, researchers and independent developers are all welcome to share ideas.

Is there a fee to participate?

There is no participation fee for selected innovations. Any additional infrastructure, third-party API, hosting or operational expenses outside the agreed support are discussed transparently before implementation.

What does “selected” mean?

Support is offered to selected projects, depending on fit with the program, technical feasibility, and available resources and availability. We’ll talk it through with you after you share your idea.

What should my idea look like?

It doesn’t need to be perfect. A clear description of the problem, your concept, and what kind of collaboration you’re looking for is enough to start a conversation.

Does my idea have to be AI-related?

No. AI is where we work, but the program is open to any innovative technology idea. If it isn’t in the listed categories, share it with us anyway.

How are details of the collaboration decided?

Scope, expectations and the details of any collaboration are discussed and agreed with you openly before implementation begins.
